About this supplement

Iron-deficiency anaemia is very common, especially in women, and shows up as tiredness, breathlessness and pale skin. Iron with folic acid is the standard treatment, and folic acid is also important in pregnancy.

Take it on an empty stomach if you can tolerate it, or with food if it upsets your stomach. Vitamin C helps absorption, while tea and calcium reduce it. Do not be alarmed if it darkens your stools — that is normal.
