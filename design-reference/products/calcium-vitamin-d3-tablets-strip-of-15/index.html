About this supplement

Calcium keeps bones and teeth strong, and Vitamin D3 helps the body actually absorb that calcium — which is why the two are combined.

This is a common daily supplement for older adults, post-menopausal women and during pregnancy on a doctor's advice. Take it with water after a meal for best absorption, and space it apart from iron tablets if you take those too.
