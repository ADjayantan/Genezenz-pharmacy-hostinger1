About this medicine

Azithromycin is a widely used antibiotic for certain chest, throat, sinus and skin infections. It is typically a short three-to-five day course.

Antibiotics only work against bacterial infections, not colds or flu, and must be taken exactly as prescribed — finish the full course even if you feel better, to avoid resistance. This is a prescription-only medicine; our pharmacist verifies your prescription before dispatch.
