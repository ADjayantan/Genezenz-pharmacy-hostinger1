About this product

ORS replaces the water and electrolytes the body loses during diarrhoea, vomiting, or heavy sweating in hot weather. It is one of the most effective and least expensive medicines there is.

Dissolve one sachet in the volume of clean water stated on the pack — no more, no less, as a stronger solution can make dehydration worse. Sip it steadily rather than drinking it all at once.

For a young child or an elderly person who cannot keep fluids down, seek medical help rather than managing at home.
