About this medicine

Cetirizine is a second-generation antihistamine used for allergic rhinitis, hay fever, hives and itching.

It causes less drowsiness than older antihistamines, but it can still make some people sleepy — be careful driving until you know how it affects you. Usually taken once daily, ideally in the evening.
