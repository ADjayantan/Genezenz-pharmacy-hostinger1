What makes a valid prescription?

A prescription photo should clearly show the doctor's name and registration, the date, your name, and the medicines with their strength and dosage. Blurry or partial photos slow things down, so a well-lit, flat photo of the whole page works best. If you are re-ordering a regular medicine, sending your previous bill helps us match exactly what you had before.
