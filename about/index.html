Who we are

Genezenz Pharmacy opened its counter in Ganapathy in 2014 with a simple idea: a local chemist should know its customers by name, and should be somewhere you can trust with your family's health. More than a decade later, that is still how we work — only now you can reach us online as well as in person.

We stock over 500 medicines and healthcare products, from everyday antipyretics and antibiotics to specialised diabetes care, cardiac medication, paediatric syrups and dermatology products. If we do not have something on the shelf, we will source it for you.

A pharmacist behind every order

Every prescription order is reviewed by a licensed pharmacist before it leaves our counter. We check the dosage, look for interactions, and call you if anything on the prescription needs clarification from your doctor. We never substitute a medicine — a different brand or strength — without speaking to you first.

That extra care is the whole point of buying from a real pharmacy rather than an anonymous warehouse. For a customer managing diabetes, blood pressure or a child's fever, it makes a genuine difference.
